# Competitive Intelligence Report: Tesla as of October 05, 2026

## 1. Executive Summary
- Tesla continues to be a leader in the electric vehicle (EV) market, although it faces increasing competition from BYD, Rivian, and Ford. 
- The company reported strong financial performance in Q2 2026, with significant revenues and vehicle deliveries, but its net income showed a slight decline compared to the previous year.
- Tesla's strategic investments in AI and energy storage are expected to enhance its competitive position, despite challenges from rivals.

## 2. Financial Performance
- **Total Revenues**: $28,236 million for the Three Months Ended June 30, 2026, compared to $22,496 million for the Three Months Ended June 30, 2025.
- **GAAP Gross Margin**: 16.8% for the Three Months Ended June 30, 2026, compared to 17.2% for the Three Months Ended June 30, 2025.
- **Net Income Attributable to Common Stockholders (GAAP)**: $1,114 million for the Three Months Ended June 30, 2026, compared to $1,172 million for the Three Months Ended June 30, 2025.
- **Free Cash Flow**: $5,762 million for the Three Months Ended June 30, 2026, compared to $6,780 million for the Three Months Ended June 30, 2025.
- **Vehicle Deliveries**: 480,126 for the Three Months Ended June 30, 2026, compared to 466,140 for the Three Months Ended June 30, 2025.
- **Energy Storage Deployed**: 22.3 GWh for the Three Months Ended June 30, 2026, compared to 18.5 GWh for the Three Months Ended June 30, 2025. ## 3. Competitive Landscape
- **BYD**: Delivered 557,090 battery-electric vehicles in Q2 2026, surpassing Tesla's deliveries by 76,964 units. [https://electriccarsreport.com/2026/07/byd-overtakes-tesla-again-in-q2-2026-ev-sales-as-global-gap-widens]
- **Rivian**: Delivered 12,194 vehicles in Q2 2026, marking a 14% increase from the previous year. [https://rivian.com/newsroom/article/rivian-releases-second-quarter-2026-financial-results]
- **Ford**: Sold 549,200 vehicles in Q2 2026, reflecting a 10% decline compared to the same period last year, with a significant drop in pure EV sales. [https://s205.q4cdn.com/882619693/files/doc_news/2026/Jul/02/Ford-U.S.-Q2-2026-Sales-Release.pdf]

## 4. Recent Developments
- **July 2, 2026**: Tesla reported Q2 2026 deliveries of 480,126 vehicles, a slight increase from the previous year. [https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/07/20/byd-delivered-557090-battery-electric-vehicles-in]
- **August 2026**: Tesla announced plans to ramp up production of its energy storage products, aiming for increased market penetration. [https://www.electrek.co/2026/07/01/byd-tesla-q2-2026-bev-sales]
- **September 2026**: Tesla's stock price fluctuated due to competitive pressures from BYD and Rivian, which reported strong delivery numbers. [https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/rivian-q2-2026-deliveries-beat-132612754.html]

## 5. SWOT Analysis
### Strengths
- Strong brand recognition and market leadership in the EV sector.
- Significant investments in AI and energy storage technologies. [TSLA-Q2-2026-Update.pdf, p. 1]

### Weaknesses
- Declining net income in Q2 2026 compared to the previous year. [TSLA-Q2-2026-Update.pdf, p. 30]
- Increased competition from established automakers and new entrants. [https://electriccarsreport.com/2026/07/byd-overtakes-tesla-again-in-q2-2026-ev-sales-as-global-gap-widens]

### Opportunities
- Expansion into new markets and segments, particularly in energy storage. [TSLA-Q2-2026-Update.pdf, p. 37]
- Potential for growth in autonomous vehicle technology and services. [TSLA-Q2-2026-Update.pdf, p. 1]

### Threats
- Intense competition from BYD, Rivian, and Ford, which may impact market share. [https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/07/20/byd-delivered-557090-battery-electric-vehicles-in]
- Regulatory changes and tariffs affecting production costs. [TSLA-Q2-2026-Update.pdf, p. 37]

## 6. Key Risks to Watch
- **Market Competition**: Increasing competition from BYD and Rivian could erode Tesla's market share and pricing power. [https://electriccarsreport.com/2026/07/byd-overtakes-tesla-again-in-q2-2026-ev-sales-as-global-gap-widens]
- **Supply Chain Disruptions**: Ongoing supply chain issues may impact production capabilities and costs. [TSLA-Q2-2026-Update.pdf, p. 37]
- **Regulatory Environment**: Changes in government incentives for EVs could affect demand and profitability. [TSLA-Q2-2026-Update.pdf, p. 37]
